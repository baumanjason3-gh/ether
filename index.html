Our Story

Clothing that exists between
the material and the immaterial.

Ether and Thread was founded on the belief that what you wear is a conversation between your body and the world. We work exclusively with natural fibres — linen, silk, undyed wool — sourced from cooperatives we visit ourselves.

Our atelier operates in small, seasonal batches. Nothing is mass-produced. Every seam is considered. We believe in the slow accumulation of a wardrobe: fewer pieces, more meaning.
